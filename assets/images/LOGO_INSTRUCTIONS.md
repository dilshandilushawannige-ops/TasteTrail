# Logo Upload Instructions

## Add Your Logo

1. Save your logo file as `logo.png`
2. Place it in this folder: `assets/images/logo.png`
3. The logo should be:
   - PNG format with transparent background
   - Approximately 300-400px wide
   - 80-100px tall
   - The Taste Trail logo with icon
